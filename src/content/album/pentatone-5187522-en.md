## Painting with the Piano

Some people paint with a brush, some with words. Enrique Granados painted with the piano—evoking the entire world of the Spanish painter Francisco Goya.

<!-- fiction: opening metaphor -->

Goyescas, Granados's greatest piano work, comes in two books of six pieces, all inspired by Goya's paintings and the street life of Madrid.

<!-- fact: Goyescas is in two books of six pieces, inspired by Goya's paintings, composed 1909–1911 -->

Here are the balls of the aristocracy, the clamor of the market, the plucking of guitars, the loneliness of late night, and the famous, melancholy "The Maiden and the Nightingale."

<!-- fact: The second book includes 'El amor y la muerte' and 'Epílogo'; 'The Maiden and the Nightingale' is a celebrated piece -->

And the pianist bringing these sounds to us is the Taiwanese-American Charlotte Hu.

<!-- fact: Pentatone PTC 5187 522, released 2026, featuring Hu's complete Goyescas -->

## The Maiden and the Nightingale

"The Maiden and the Nightingale" (Quejas, o la Maja y el Ruiseñor) is the emotional heart of the whole set.

<!-- fact: 'Quejas, o la Maja y el Ruiseñor' is the first piece of the second book, depicting a maiden confiding in a nightingale -->

Granados imitates the nightingale's song here—not realistically, but with those ornaments, laden with Gypsy melancholy, that are so characteristic of Spanish music.

<!-- fiction: description of the musical device -->

Hu's touch makes the melody seem to hang in mid-air, so light it almost vanishes, yet always within reach. It speaks of a Spanish girl's secret sorrows.

<!-- fiction: description of the performance -->

## From Madrid to Taipei

Granados lived his life in the light and shadow of Madrid. And Hu, setting out from Taiwan, carried this music to the world's stages.

<!-- fiction: contrast and setup -->

As a Taiwanese-American pianist, Hu has performed at Taipei's National Concert Hall—so this Goyescas recording carries a special meaning for Taiwanese listeners: a musician from Taiwan, interpreting the soul of Spain.

<!-- fact: Charlotte Hu is Taiwanese-American and has performed at Taipei's National Concert Hall -->
