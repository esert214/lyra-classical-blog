## Two Faces of One Swan

By day she is Odette—pure, sorrowful, a cursed swan princess. By night she becomes Odile—the black swan, seductive, lethal, gleaming with wicked light.

<!-- fact: The ballerina dances both the White Swan Odette and the Black Swan Odile -->

One dancer, two souls. This is Swan Lake's ultimate test for a ballerina.

And in this live video from January 25, 2015 at Moscow's Bolshoi Theatre, the one taking that test is Svetlana Zakharova.

<!-- fact: This recording is live from January 25, 2015 at the Bolshoi Theatre; Zakharova dances Odette/Odile -->

## Why Zakharova

If you can watch only one dancer perform Swan Lake, many would say: watch Zakharova.

<!-- opinion: subjective assessment -->

Her arms are real wings—every sweep carries wind. When she turns into the Black Swan, even the angle of her spine spells arrogance. Critics call her "the most beautiful Odette of our time"—not flattery, but physical description.

<!-- fiction: literary description of her dancing -->

Denis Rodkin, the Bolshoi's leading man of the new generation, is her partner; their pas de deux is the heart of this version.

<!-- fact: Denis Rodkin is a leading principal of the Bolshoi -->

## The Grigorovich Version

This is choreographer Yuri Grigorovich's version—created for the Bolshoi in 1969, fusing the classical skeleton of Petipa and Ivanov with Soviet-era drama.

<!-- fact: The choreography is Yuri Grigorovich's version (premiered 1969), following the Petipa/Ivanov tradition -->

It has a distinctive ending: Siegfried battles the Evil Genius and fails—love cannot conquer fate. Colder than the "happy ending" versions. More Russian.

<!-- fact: The Grigorovich version ends tragically, unlike some Western happy-ending versions -->

## The Bolshoi and Taiwan

The name Bolshoi appeared in Taiwan long ago.

<!-- fact: Bolshoi dancers visited Taipei in 1993 -->

In June 1993, the "Bolshoi Ballet Stars" took the stage at Taipei's Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall to dance Act II of Swan Lake—with the legendary prima Bessmertnova herself. Thirty years before this DVD, Taiwanese audiences had already met the real Bolshoi.

<!-- fact: June 2–5, 1993: Bolshoi Ballet Stars performed Swan Lake Act II at Taipei's Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall -->

## Why Collect It

If you can't fly to Moscow, this DVD is the closest you'll get to the Bolshoi stage.
