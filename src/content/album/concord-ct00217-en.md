## The Price of Staying Young

"Death Becomes Her"—death, becomes her.

<!-- fiction: opening reference to the title -->

This musical is adapted from the classic 1992 comedy film of the same name, about two women who drink a magic potion to stay young forever.

<!-- fact: The musical is adapted from the 1992 comedy film, about two women who seek eternal youth -->

But this is not a simple "fairy tale." It is a witty, satirical black comedy—about beauty, jealousy, and the absurd lengths people go to avoid growing old.

<!-- fiction: interpretation of the work's nature -->

## Two Leading Ladies

Megan Hilty plays Madeline—the vain, self-centered faded star. Jennifer Simard plays Helen—the bookish woman Madeline stole everything from.

<!-- fact: Megan Hilty plays Madeline Ashton; Jennifer Simard plays Helen Sharp -->

Both are Broadway comedy masters. Hilty's voice is glorious, rendering Madeline's "elegant on the surface, frantic within" quality to the full; Simard's timing and physical comedy turn Helen from a pitiful creature into an avenging goddess.

<!-- fiction: commentary on the two leads -->

## Earworm Melodies

This soundtrack's music is hailed as "highly catchy." Those singable choruses will stay in your head for days.

<!-- fact: The show's music is praised as witty and catchy -->

Julia Mattison and Noel Carey's songs are full of Broadway wit and satire. Numbers like "For the Gaze" wrap sharp critique in gorgeous melody—about women, beauty, and the anxiety of aging.
