## Stripping Away Hollywood Antique Chandelier Clutter

Who could have predicted that Andrew Lloyd Webber’s opulent 1993 classic—once synonymous with gilded staircases and rococo excess—would be stripped down to black-and-white psychological brutality?

Directed by theatrical visionary Jamie Lloyd and starring former Pussycat Dolls frontwoman Nicole Scherzinger, this radical revival swept **seven Olivier Awards** in London before conquering Broadway in an absolute box-office frenzy. Recorded live, this cast album bottles Scherzinger's volcanic, once-in-a-generation vocal tour-de-force.

## "I Am Big! It’s the Pictures That Got Small!"

Scherzinger discards the theatrical mannerisms of Gloria Swanson or Glenn Close. Blending athletic vocal belting, raw rock snarl, and peerless technical control, her Norma Desmond is a feral predator trapped in the funhouse mirror of fame.

In *With One Look*, she proclaims silent-screen supremacy with imperious swagger. Later, in *As If We Never Said Goodbye*, as Norma re-enters Paramount Studios, Scherzinger traces an astonishing arc from tremulous, childlike vulnerability to a stratospheric, roof-raising climax that detonates applause like rolling thunder.

## Stark Shadows at Wally Records

This is a recording that tests every dynamic limit of your audio system.

Stripped of syrupy strings, Jack Knowles’s minimalist arrangements let synth pulses and driving percussion hammer directly into your chest. Scherzinger proves that a true superstar requires no ornate costumes or sets: place her in a shaft of white spotlight with a microphone, and the universe bows.
