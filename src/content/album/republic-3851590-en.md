## Reclaiming History with Fierce Pop Crowns

"Divorced, Beheaded, Died, Divorced, Beheaded, Survived"—for five centuries, the six wives of Henry VIII were relegated to tragic footnotes in history textbooks, defined solely by how the Tudor tyrant disposed of them.

Then two Cambridge students, Toby Marlow and Lucy Moss, turned history on its head with *SIX*. The six queens grab wireless microphones as Beyoncé, Avril Lavigne, and Rihanna-style pop divas, staging a fierce concert showdown to decide who suffered the most and deserves to be frontwoman of the band!

<!-- fact: Premiered at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe in 2017, SIX went on to conquer London’s West End and Broadway, winning the 2022 Tony Award for Best Original Score -->

## 80 Minutes of Relentless Pop-Euphoria

The opening track *Ex-Wives* drops a heavy electronic dance beat announcing that the history books are being rewritten. Anne Boleyn skewers patriarchal paranoia in the bubblegum-punk anthem *Don't Lose Ur Head*, while Jane Seymour delivers heart-wrenching emotional fortitude in the soaring power ballad *Heart of Stone*.

By the euphoric finale *Six*, the queens realize that competing for the spotlight of their dead husband is a fool's errand. Tearing up the royal script, they sing their own unapologetic "Her-story."

Turn up the volume and let this record blast. It possesses a transformative magic that incinerates self-doubt. It screams: never let anyone else dictate your worth—take the mic, step into the light, and rule your own kingdom.
