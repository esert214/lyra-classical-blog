## A Half-Century of Games

"Játékok" is Hungarian for "games."

<!-- fact: Játékok is Hungarian for 'games' -->

But Kurtág's Játékok is not ordinary children's pieces. It is a collection of over a thousand piano pieces spanning half a century, hailed as "one of the most important piano collections of the half-century."

<!-- fact: Játékok is Kurtág's vast piano collection spanning half a century, hailed as among the most important piano works of the half-century -->

Under the name of "games," Kurtág experiments with the piano's many possibilities—minimalist notes, huge leaps, contrasts of silence and sound.

<!-- fiction: description of Játékok's musical language -->

## Silence and Sound

Kurtág's music often hovers between minimalism and silence. In Játékok, there are almost inaudible pianissimos, and sudden fortissimo explosions—like a game about "whether sound exists or not."

<!-- fiction: description of Kurtág's style -->

With his matchless touch, Aimard draws enormous energy from these minimalist notes. You are drawn in by the quiet moments, and startled by the sudden bursts.

<!-- fiction: commentary on Aimard's playing -->
