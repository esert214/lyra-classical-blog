## A Historic Vindication Forty-Two Years in the Making

In November 1981, Stephen Sondheim and director Hal Prince’s *Merrily We Roll Along* closed after a disastrous run of sixteen performances, marking the most painful heartbreak of Sondheim’s legendary career.

Yet masterpieces refuse to stay buried. In the 2023–2024 Broadway season, Maria Friedman’s definitive revival swept **four Tony Awards**, including Best Revival of a Musical, Best Actor, and Best Featured Actor. Preserved on this Sony Masterworks cast album, the production stands as a long-overdue triumph for Sondheim’s most misunderstood gem.

## The Heartbreak of Reverse Chronology

The structural brilliance of *Merrily* lies in its backward march through time. Opening in 1976 with bitter, cynical middle-aged estrangement, the story peels away two decades of compromise until it arrives in 1957 on a quiet Manhattan rooftop—where three penniless dreamers look up at Sputnik, believing their bond will endure forever.

Daniel Radcliffe delivers a tour-de-force breakdown in *Franklin Shepard, Inc.*, spitting frantic lyrics with neurotic virtuosity as friendship curdles into television humiliation. Opposite him, Jonathan Groff imbues Franklin with heartbreaking nuance, capturing the seductive glide into creative compromise through tunes like *Good Thing Going* and *Old Friends*.

## Rooftop Satellites Over Wally Records

When the final rooftop anthem *Our Time* swells, the youthful trio harmonizes in naive exaltation: "It's our time, coming through, all our dreams coming true..."

Knowing the wreckage that awaits them makes their youthful sincerity devastating. We were all once those dreamers on the roof. As the vinyl spins backward into innocent memory, it hands us back a piece of our former selves.
