## The Man Trapped Underground

1925, Kentucky, USA. Caver Floyd Collins was trapped in a narrow cave for 17 days, until the end of his life.

<!-- fact: Floyd Collins was trapped in a cave in Kentucky in 1925 and died after 17 days -->

The event gripped the nation—thousands gathered at the cave mouth, reporters and photographers swarmed, staging a battle between media and life.

<!-- fact: The event gripped the nation, sparking a media frenzy -->

And this musical, Floyd Collins, turns that true tragedy into a profound work about media, humanity, and life.

<!-- fact: The musical adapts the true story of Floyd Collins -->

## Hailed as an Artistic Benchmark

With music and lyrics by Adam Guettel, this musical is hailed as "the artistic benchmark of contemporary musical theatre."

<!-- fact: The musical, with music and lyrics by Adam Guettel, is hailed as the artistic benchmark of contemporary musical theatre -->

Guettel is the grandson of composer Richard Rodgers, but his musical style is entirely different from his grandfather's—more complex, more modern, with a distinctive lyricism and uncertainty.

<!-- fact: Guettel is the grandson of Richard Rodgers; his style is modern and distinctive -->

That song "The Riddle Song" captures, with an almost impossible melody, Floyd's confusion and hope in the dark cave.

<!-- fact: 'The Riddle Song' is a famous song in the show -->

## Media and Life

Floyd Collins's most profound aspect is its critique of media.

<!-- fiction: interpretation of the work's theme -->

Floyd is trapped underground, while the media above consumes his suffering—reporting, sensationalizing, waiting for his death. This musical, premiered in 1996, has become more satirical and pointed than ever in today's social media age.

<!-- fact: The musical premiered in 1996, exploring the battle between media and life -->

## Why Listen

Jeremy Jordan's Floyd is the core of the show. His performance on a cramped stage makes the audience feel as if they too are inside that dark cave.

<!-- fiction: commentary on Jordan's performance -->

This 2025 revival soundtrack preserves Jordan's heartbreaking interpretation. It is both an outstanding musical album and a profound reflection on humanity and media.
