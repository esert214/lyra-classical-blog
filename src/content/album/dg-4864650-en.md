## Zimerman's Chamber Music

When we think of Krystian Zimerman, we think of his glorious solo career—the 1975 Chopin Competition gold medal, the famous collaborations with Bernstein.

<!-- fact: Zimerman is the 1975 Chopin Competition gold medalist -->

But Zimerman is equally devoted to chamber music. On this album, he forms a quartet with three young musicians to record Brahms's Piano Quartets.

<!-- fact: This album features Brahms's piano quartets, played by Zimerman with three musicians -->

The Zimerman-led ensemble makes every part of this album sound like a conversation.

<!-- opinion: commentary on the album's performance character -->

## Brahms's Passion

Brahms wrote three piano quartets in his life. They are not "piano with string accompaniment," but true chamber music of four equal instruments.

<!-- fact: Brahms's three piano quartets are true chamber music of four equal instruments -->

The Second Piano Quartet is especially known for its passion. Critics praise this recording for "a dialogue-like rapport between instruments."

<!-- fact: The Second Piano Quartet is known for passion; this recording is praised for dialogue-like rapport -->

Zimerman's piano neither overpowers the strings nor recedes into the background. His rapport with violin, viola, and cello lets Brahms's complex counterpoint and emotion flow naturally.

<!-- fiction: commentary on the performance -->

## The Intimacy of Chamber Music

Unlike the grandeur of symphonies, chamber music emphasizes intimacy and dialogue.

<!-- opinion: commentary on chamber music's character -->

This album lets you hear four top musicians in close conversation. No conductor, no audience applause—only four souls communicating through music.

<!-- fiction: description of the listening experience -->

## Why Listen

Released by DG in 2025, this is among the finest Brahms piano quartet recordings of recent years.

<!-- fact: DG 4864650, released 2025 -->

If you love Brahms, or want to see Zimerman's skill beyond the solo repertoire, this album is worth collecting.
