## A Musical Dream from Havana

In 1997, American guitarist Ry Cooder gathered a group of forgotten veteran musicians in Havana, Cuba, to record an album—Buena Vista Social Club.

<!-- fact: Ry Cooder recorded the Buena Vista Social Club album in Havana in 1997 -->

That album made these Cuban legends visible to the world again, turning "Buena Vista Social Club" into a world-music legend.

<!-- fact: The album made Cuban musicians visible again; Buena Vista Social Club became a world-music legend -->

And this 2025 Broadway musical brings that legend to the stage—it is also the first Broadway musical performed entirely in Spanish.

<!-- fact: This musical is Broadway's first performed entirely in Spanish -->

## The All-Spanish Breakthrough

When Broadway stages a fully Spanish-language musical for the first time, that itself is a major event.

<!-- opinion: commentary on its significance -->

The show does not treat Spanish as an "exotic" decoration; it makes language a part of the music itself—Cuban rhythms, Havana flavor, the stories of those old musicians, all told in their mother tongue.

<!-- fiction: interpretation of the work's handling of language -->

The band behind it earned a 2025 Special Tony Award, recognizing the work's cultural and musical breakthrough.

<!-- fact: The band earned a 2025 Special Tony Award -->

## The Legendary Sound

This soundtrack features the show's songs, blending traditional Cuban son, bolero, and jazz.

<!-- fact: The soundtrack blends Cuban son, bolero, and jazz styles -->

Those melodies carry Havana's characteristic languor and passion. Even if you don't understand Spanish, you feel the music's vitality.

<!-- fiction: commentary on the music -->

## A Taiwanese Regret

Interestingly, this work has an unfinished story with Taiwan. The Buena Vista Social Club ensemble was scheduled to perform at Taipei's National Concert Hall in March 2016, but the concert was canceled because the Cuban government cited political reasons, and it never took place.

<!-- fact: The Buena Vista Social Club ensemble's March 2016 Taipei concert was canceled due to political reasons by the Cuban government -->

Though the ensemble never reached Taiwan, through this musical and its soundtrack, that Havana passion has finally crossed the ocean by another route.

<!-- fiction: emotional conclusion about the Taiwan connection -->

## Why Listen

In 2026, this soundtrack won the Grammy Award for Best Musical Theater Album.

<!-- fact: This soundtrack won the 2026 Grammy for Best Musical Theater Album -->

This is an album that lets you feel both the passion of Cuban music and the narrative charm of Broadway. It crosses boundaries of language and culture, proving that music itself is the best translation.

<!-- opinion: subjective recommendation -->
