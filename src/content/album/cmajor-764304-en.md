## Salzburg’s Gramophone Opera of the Year

Among twentieth-century operatic tragedies, Leoš Janáček’s *Káťa Kabanová* shines like a diamond forged under unbearable emotional pressure.

Documenting the triumph of the 2022 Salzburg Festival, this C Major release captured the **Gramophone Opera Award**. Under the baton of Czech maestro Jakub Hrůša—Music Director Designate of the Royal Opera House, Covent Garden—the Vienna Philharmonic bathes Janáček’s jagged, speech-inflected rhythms in the world’s most opulent, warm string colors.

<!-- fact: Recorded live at the Salzburg Festival (C Major 764304), this production won the Gramophone Opera Award, conducted by Jakub Hrůša with Corinne Winters starring as Káťa -->

## Trapped Wings by the Cold Volga

American soprano Corinne Winters delivers a performance of shattering vulnerability. In a suffocating nineteenth-century provincial Russian town dominated by her tyrannical mother-in-law and spineless husband, Káťa dreams only of growing wings like a bird and soaring into the clouds.

Winters portrays Káťa not as a grand operatic diva, but as a wounded, exposed nerve. In the storm scene of Act III, as the Vienna brass and timpani summon apocalyptic fury, Káťa confesses her adultery before plunging into the icy depths of the Volga River.

## Echoes of the Volga at Wally Records

Patrons unfamiliar with Czech repertoire often hesitate when encountering Janáček. But once Hrůša's orchestra surges forward, resistance dissolves.

Janáček wastes no time on ornamental filler. His music cuts like a scalpel directly into the heart's tenderest core. As the faint cello murmur concludes the final scene, a quiet chill lingers over our record shelves.
