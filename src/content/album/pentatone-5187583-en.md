## Is That a Human Voice?

Everyone hearing a countertenor for the first time asks the same question.

<!-- fiction: opening resonance -->

How can such a voice—a voice high as moonlight yet warm as a chest—live inside a man's body?

John Holiday's debut album Over My Head is exactly that voice, singing an entire album about faith, love, and hope.

<!-- fact: Over My Head is John Holiday's debut solo album on Pentatone (PTC 5187583), released July 17, 2026; recorded November 2024 at WGBH studios, Boston -->

## Only Songs That Mean Something

Holiday said it plainly in an interview: "I told Kevin—my pianist, my best friend—I didn't want to include anything that doesn't speak to my heart, mind and spirit. Because it has to mean something to me in order to mean something to the audience."

<!-- fact: In his Presto interview Holiday explained that every track had to speak to his heart, mind and spirit -->

So this album breaks all the rules. It is not a typical Baroque countertenor recital—it holds the spiritual "Over My Head," his own arrangement of "Amazing Grace," art songs by Leslie Adams and Robert Morris, and two world-premiere recordings by Theo Morrison and Carlos Simon.

<!-- fact: The album includes spirituals, art songs, jazz standards, and world premieres by Morrison and Simon -->

Even "Fly Me to the Moon" and Gershwin's "Summertime" are here.

<!-- fact: The album also includes Bart Howard's 'Fly Me to the Moon' and Gershwin's 'Summertime' -->

## The Heaviest Song

The one that stops your breath is "Strange Fruit."

<!-- fiction: personal listening highlight -->

Abel Meeropol's protest song about the dark history of lynching in the American South—Billie Holiday sang it, Nina Simone sang it. And now John Holiday sings it in the countertenor range, those high notes like cries carried from very far away.

<!-- fact: 'Strange Fruit' was written by Abel Meeropol and stands as a famous protest song in American civil-rights history -->

The New York Times named this among the best classical albums of August 2026; Presto's reviewer called it some of the most jaw-dropping singing he'd heard all year.

<!-- fact: The album was praised by the New York Times (Aug 2026) and Presto called it among the most jaw-dropping singing of the year -->

## Taiwan Remembers Him

Taiwanese audiences already know this voice.

<!-- fact: Holiday performed in Taiwan in 2018 -->

In late 2018, composer Huang Ruo's chamber opera Paradise Interrupted came to Taiwan for its local premiere—the opening season of Weiwuying, followed by added performances at the National Theater in Taipei. Holiday sang First Male Voice, the voice singing at the edge of a dream.

<!-- fact: In late 2018 Paradise Interrupted played Weiwuying's opening season and Taipei's National Theater, with Holiday as First Male Voice -->

If you were there, you've already heard him.
