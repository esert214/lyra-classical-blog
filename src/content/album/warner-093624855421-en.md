## Steam and Blood Beneath the Victorian London Fog

Following the passing of Stephen Sondheim in late 2021, Broadway mounted its grandest tribute: the 2023 mega-revival of *Sweeney Todd* at the Lunt-Fontanne Theatre.

Shunning budget-slashed chamber reductions, this production boldly restored Jonathan Tunick’s original 26-piece symphonic orchestration. When the bone-rattling brass screech and factory organ roar in *The Ballad of Sweeney Todd*, the merciless, human-grinding maw of the Industrial Revolution slams into the listener.

## Fire and Ice: Groban Meets Ashford

Vocal superstar Josh Groban delivers the performance of his career. Casting aside his velvet balladeer persona, he hardens his rich baritone into cold steel. In the terrifying centerpiece *Epiphany*, his descent from grief to genocidal madness ("We all deserve to die!") chills the listener to the bone.

Playing opposite him, Annaleigh Ashford’s Mrs. Lovett is a comic marvel. In *A Little Priest*, her eccentric, physical timing turns the macabre idea of baking clergymen and politicians into meat pies into an uproarious, deliciously twisted waltz.

<!-- fact: Premiered in 1979, Sweeney Todd won eight Tony Awards and is regarded as Sondheim's operatic masterpiece -->

## Crushed Beneath the Gears of Injustice

What elevates Sondheim’s melodrama into timeless tragedy is its burning indictment of social cruelty. The demon barber of Fleet Street is not born an evil monster—he is forged by a corrupt legal system that stole his life. The razor is merely a mirror reflecting a broken world.
