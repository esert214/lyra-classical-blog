## A Mountain Picnic of Four Virtuoso Giants

"Goat Rodeo" is aviation slang for a chaotic situation where a million things must go right simultaneously to avert disaster. In 2011, Yo-Yo Ma joined forces with double bass titan Edgar Meyer, bluegrass fiddle wizard Stuart Duncan, and mandolin phenom Chris Thile, creating a crossover sensation that swept two Grammy Awards.

Nine years later, the four old comrades regrouped for the sequel: *Not Our First Goat Rodeo*. Relaxed, fearless, and telepathically linked, they dive into uncharted acoustic terrain.

<!-- fact: Released in June 2020 by Sony Masterworks, featuring guest vocals by singer-songwriter Aoife O'Donovan -->

## Baroque Counterpoint Meets Bluegrass Swagger

This is no superficial pop crossover. In the dizzying opener *Your Coffee Is a Disaster*, Meyer’s double bass provides buoyant rhythmic architecture, Thile’s mandolin dances at blinding velocity, while Ma’s 1712 Stradivari cello weaves rich, Bach-like contrapuntal tapestries.

The four string voices chase, interrupt, and celebrate one another. One moment you are sitting on a porch in the Blue Ridge Mountains listening to a breakdown; the next, you are inside a high-classical chamber hall marveling at modern voice-leading.

<!-- fact: Ma and Meyer’s collaboration dates back to the seminal 1996 album Appalachia Waltz -->

## Dismantling the Walls of Genre

We love boxing art into neat categories: classical, folk, jazz. But records like this expose the absurdity of genre gates. When master musicians converse with uninhibited joy, music becomes an open meadow welcoming all.
