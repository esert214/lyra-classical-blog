## Thirty-Eight Planes

September 11, 2001. American airspace closes.

<!-- fact: After 9/11 US airspace closed completely and all international flights were diverted -->

Thirty-eight passenger jets bound for the United States are forced to land somewhere most people have never heard of—Gander, Newfoundland, Canada.

<!-- fact: On September 11, thirty-eight flights were diverted to Gander International Airport in Newfoundland -->

The town holds fewer than ten thousand people. That day, it suddenly gained almost seven thousand strangers.

<!-- fact: Gander's population is about ten thousand; roughly seven thousand passengers arrived -->

The locals call these travelers "come from away"—outsiders. This musical tells what happened over the next five days.

<!-- fact: 'Come From Away' is a Newfoundland expression meaning outsiders; the story is based on true events -->

## Me and the Sky

The most moving solo on this album is "Me and the Sky."

<!-- fact: 'Me and the Sky' is Beverley Bass's solo song -->

It belongs to a real person: Beverley Bass—one of American Airlines' first female captains. She fought her way into the cockpit all her life, and on that day in 2001, her flight was one of the thirty-eight.

<!-- fact: Beverley Bass was one of AA's first female captains; her flight was diverted to Gander on 9/11 -->

When Jenn Colella sings it, you hear an entire life—from a mocked little girl to a captain gripping the controls—then gently landing at the song's end.

<!-- fiction: literary description of the performance -->

Colella earned a Tony nomination and a Drama Desk Award for the role.

<!-- fact: Jenn Colella received a Tony nomination and Drama Desk Award for this role -->

## Twelve Actors, a Hundred Souls

The staging is minimal: rows of chairs become the cabin, a bar, a church.

<!-- fact: The show famously uses chairs to transform scenes -->

And only twelve actors fill the stage—playing nearly one hundred roles: captains, mayors, reporters, cooks, terrified passengers.

<!-- fact: Twelve actors play close to one hundred characters -->

The band sits onstage playing Newfoundland folk—jigs and rhythms carried by Irish immigrants—so even the saddest moments keep walking with warmth.

<!-- fact: The score fuses Newfoundland folk with Irish traditional styles -->

## Taiwan Doesn't Know Its Name Yet

Come From Away won four Olivier Awards including Best New Musical, plus Tony Best Director—yet has never been staged in Taiwan.

<!-- fact: The show won four Olivier Awards (2019, including Best New Musical) and Tony Best Director among its nominations (2017) -->

Its nearest Asian footprints are Seoul and Tokyo. But through the Apple TV+ filmed version, Taiwanese audiences have long known Gander.

<!-- fact: An Apple TV+ filmed version exists (2021); Asian stagings include Seoul, Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya -->

In an age full of divisive news, this is a work that makes you believe strangers can be saviors.
