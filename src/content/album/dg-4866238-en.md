## The Father of the Nocturne

When we say "nocturne," we think of Chopin. But did you know that, before Chopin, a composer had already created this form?

<!-- fiction: opening question -->

That composer is the Irish composer John Field. It was he who invented the nocturne—that beautiful, lyrical form of piano music.

<!-- fact: John Field is the inventor of the nocturne -->

And this album by Alice Sara Ott is a complete recording of Field's nocturnes—the complete gems of the "father of the nocturne."

<!-- fact: This album is a complete recording of Field's nocturnes, by Alice Sara Ott -->

## The Beauty Before Chopin

Field's nocturnes are the most beautiful piano nocturne tradition before Chopin.

<!-- fact: Field's nocturnes are the most beautiful piano nocturne tradition before Chopin -->

They are not as dramatic, not as intense, as Chopin's. They are more plain, more gentle, carrying an early, pure beauty.

<!-- opinion: commentary on the difference between Field and Chopin -->

Field's melodies are simple and flowing; those ornaments and songlike melodies directly inspired the later Chopin.

<!-- fact: Field's songlike nocturne melodies inspired Chopin -->

## Ott's Interpretation

Alice Sara Ott is the soul of this album. The German-Japanese pianist has toured with Field's nocturnes as a theme in recent years.

<!-- fact: Alice Sara Ott has toured with Field's nocturnes as a theme in recent years -->

Her interpretation preserves the purity and simplicity of Field's music, while giving it a modern warmth and sensibility. She makes these "old" nocturnes sound fresh and moving.

<!-- opinion: commentary on Ott's interpretation -->

Even while living with illness, Ott continues to perform—this persistence and love of music gives her playing added expressiveness.

<!-- opinion: commentary on Ott's background and playing -->

## Why Listen

This album was named a 2025 pick by The Guardian—the best way to get to know the "father of the nocturne."

<!-- fact: This album was a 2025 pick by The Guardian -->

If you love Chopin's nocturnes but have never heard their source, this album returns you to where it all began. It is a beautiful album suited for late-night listening, letting the soul settle.

<!-- opinion: subjective recommendation -->
