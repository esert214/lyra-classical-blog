## Silver Bells on a Modern Steinway

Following his sensational Chopin Competition triumph, Seong-Jin Cho refused to remain confined to the Romantic playground. In 2023, he unveiled an exquisite, courageous project: *The Handel Project*.

Handel's keyboard suites are too often neglected by modern concert pianists, left largely to harpsichord specialists. Yet under Cho's golden touch, the modern Steinway sheds all heavy pedaling. Instead, crystal-clear articulation evokes morning dew on spring leaves, breathing luminous poetic life into Baroque allemandes and courantes.

<!-- fact: Released by Deutsche Grammophon in February 2023, featuring Handel’s Keyboard Suites HWV 427, 430 ('The Harmonious Blacksmith'), 433, and Brahms’s Handel Variations Op. 24 -->

## From Courtly Elegance to a Titanic Fugue

The initial suites flow with courtly sunlight and crystalline ease. Then the architecture expands monumentally into Brahms’s 25 *Variations and Fugue on a Theme by Handel*, Op. 24.

Regarded as one of the most formidable variation sets ever written, Cho builds it like a soaring Gothic cathedral. In the climactic concluding fugue, intricate polyphony remains transparent as chiseled marble even amidst furious tempos and thunderous chords.
