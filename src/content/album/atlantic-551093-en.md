## One Song, an Entire Life

"How does a bastard, orphan, son of a whore and a Scotsman…"

<!-- fact: 'Alexander Hamilton' is the opening track; its first line encapsulates the protagonist's origins -->

The first minute of Hamilton throws an entire life at you with one question.

A bastard, an orphan, an immigrant from the Caribbean—how did he become the driving force of America's founding?

<!-- fact: Alexander Hamilton was a Founding Father and the first U.S. Treasury Secretary, born on Nevis in the Caribbean -->

And it's all sung in hip-hop.

## Why Rap History

Lin-Manuel Miranda read Ron Chernow's Hamilton biography on vacation—over eight hundred pages. When he finished, he thought: this is rap material.

<!-- fact: Miranda was inspired by Ron Chernow's biography of Hamilton -->

Because rap has the highest information density of any musical form. The same three minutes that carry a pop song's few dozen words can hold a novel's worth of story in a rap verse.

<!-- fiction: commentary on hip-hop's narrative efficiency -->

So the founders' debates became battles, political struggle became cyphers, and King George III sings a Beatles-style breakup song mocking the colonies.

<!-- fact: 'You'll Be Back' presents the King's perspective in a British-pop style -->

## The People History Books Skip

What makes this show truly great isn't only technique.

It lets Eliza Hamilton sing last. After her husband died, she lived another fifty years—founding New York's first private orphanage, preserving his papers, raising funds for the Washington Monument.

<!-- fact: Eliza Hamilton lived for half a century after her husband's death, founded New York's first private orphanage, and preserved his legacy -->

"Who lives, who dies, who tells your story?" That question is what the whole show hands to its audience.

<!-- fact: 'Who Lives, Who Dies, Who Tells Your Story' is the finale -->

## Taiwan Is Still Waiting

Hamilton has never been staged in Taipei.

Its first international tour went to Auckland, Manila, Abu Dhabi, Singapore—around most of Asia without stopping in Taiwan.

<!-- fact: The international tour included Auckland, Manila, Abu Dhabi, and Singapore, but no Taipei stop -->

But Taiwanese fans have long known it through Disney+'s official filmed version. Perhaps someday, that ship will dock.

<!-- fiction: hope for the future -->
