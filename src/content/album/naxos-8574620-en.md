## A Friendly Voice in Contemporary Music

For many, "contemporary music" carries a certain distance—as if it's not easy to understand or approach.

<!-- opinion: commentary on the stereotype of contemporary music -->

But Anna Clyne's music is a rare "friendly voice" in the contemporary field.

<!-- opinion: commentary on Clyne's musical character -->

Clyne is a British-American composer; her music is tonal, colorful, and structurally clear—you don't need to be a musicologist to feel its beauty.

<!-- fact: Anna Clyne is a British-American composer; her music is tonal, colorful, and structurally clear -->

## Between Abstract and Concrete

This album, titled Abstractions, contains several of Clyne's orchestral works.

<!-- fact: The album Abstractions contains orchestral works by Clyne -->

Though the title is "abstract," Clyne's music is not at all the baffling kind. On the contrary, her music always carries concrete imagery or emotion—letting abstract musical forms carry perceivable beauty.

<!-- fiction: interpretation of the relationship between title and music -->

## Alsop's Leadership

Conducting this album is Marin Alsop—a pioneer for women in American conducting and the Baltimore Symphony's first female music director.

<!-- fact: Marin Alsop is the Baltimore Symphony's first female music director -->

Alsop has deep understanding of contemporary American music. Under her baton, the Baltimore Symphony renders Clyne's colorful orchestration with both clarity and vitality.

<!-- opinion: commentary on Alsop's interpretation -->

## Why Listen

This album is an ideal introduction to contemporary music.

<!-- opinion: subjective commentary -->

If you're daunted by "modern music," Clyne's music is a friendly starting point. It has the freshness of contemporary music while retaining the beauty and listenability of tradition.

<!-- opinion: subjective recommendation -->

This is an album you can relax and enjoy, yet still feel the depth and breadth of music.

<!-- opinion: subjective recommendation -->
