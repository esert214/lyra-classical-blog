## Dawn Breaking After a Long Winter

During the darkest days of the 2020 global lockdown, when opera houses stood shuttered, reigning Metropolitan Opera prima donna Sonya Yoncheva retreated to her artistic origins: the 17th-century Baroque.

This deeply personal concept album with Sony Classical is titled *Rebirth*. Partnering with Argentinian maestro Leonardo García Alarcón and Cappella Mediterranea, Yoncheva weaves gems by Monteverdi, Cavalli, Barbara Strozzi, and John Dowland’s heartbreaking *Flow, my tears*. Spanning four centuries, the program offers a luminous antidote to modern isolation.

<!-- fact: Recorded in La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, during the pandemic shutdown and released internationally in spring 2021 -->

## From Plagues of Antiquity to Radiant Dances

Seventeenth-century Europe weathered catastrophic plagues and the Thirty Years' War, yet its music overflowed with stubborn hope. In Cavalli’s *Calisto*, Yoncheva turns mythological laments into urgent, palpitating human pulses.

In Dowland's *Flow, my tears*, accompanied only by intimate archlute and theorbo, the powerhouse diva behind Tosca and Violetta contracts her immense instrument into a whisper of smoke. Toward the end of the album, Bulgarian folk laments merge with sensual Baroque chaconnes, blossoming into ecstatic dance.

<!-- fact: Yoncheva’s career was launched under the mentorship of early music pioneer William Christie in Les Arts Florissants -->

## As Long as We Sing, Life Endures

Music has always been humanity's most resilient sanctuary. Yoncheva proves that even when the gears of society halt, as long as a bow touches gut strings and a human throat vibrates, rebirth is inevitable.
