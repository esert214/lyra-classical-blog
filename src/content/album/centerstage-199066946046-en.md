## The Power of Words

The Book Thief is Australian author Markus Zusak's bestselling novel, set in Nazi Germany.

<!-- fact: The Book Thief is Markus Zusak's bestselling novel, set in Nazi Germany -->

The protagonist is a young girl named Liesel. In an era of book-burning, she secretly collects words—stealing books, learning to read, using words to fight the darkness.

<!-- fact: The protagonist Liesel secretly collects words and books in Nazi Germany -->

And this musical brings that story of the power of words to the stage.

<!-- fact: The musical adapts the novel and premiered in the UK in 2023 -->

## From Novel to Stage

Adapting The Book Thief into a musical is a difficult task. Because the novel's narrator is not human—it is "Death" itself.

<!-- fact: The novel is narrated by 'Death' -->

And Elyssa Samsel and Kate Anderson's songs deftly balance heaviness and hope. The music carries both the shadow of Nazi Germany and the small light Liesel ignites with words.

<!-- fiction: interpretation of the songwriting style -->

## A Warm Tragedy

This musical is not a "happy" work. It confronts war, death, and the darkness of humanity.

<!-- opinion: commentary on the work's nature -->

But its core is warm—about love, friendship, and people who, in the darkness, still share a glimmer of light.

<!-- fiction: interpretation of the work's core -->

This cast recording preserves the 2023 UK premiere production. Those songs, even through a speaker, let you feel the warmth of Liesel's world.

<!-- opinion: commentary on the recording -->
