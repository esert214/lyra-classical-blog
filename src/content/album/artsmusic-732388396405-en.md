## The Greatest Mother in Musical Theatre

In musical theatre history, no mother role is more controversial than Gypsy's Mama Rose.

<!-- fact: Mama Rose of Gypsy is one of the most famous mother roles in musical theatre -->

She is domineering, controlling, projecting her unfulfilled dreams of stardom onto her two daughters. She pushes them onto the stage by any means—even at the cost of ruining their lives.

<!-- fact: Mama Rose projects her stardom dreams onto her daughters, sacrificing their lives -->

But this "villain mother" is impossible to completely dislike. Because Sondheim and Styne gave her, through music, a heartbreaking complexity.

<!-- opinion: commentary on the character -->

## McDonald, Six-Time Tony Winner

Playing Mama Rose is six-time Tony Award winner Audra McDonald.

<!-- fact: Audra McDonald, a six-time Tony Award winner, plays Mama Rose -->

McDonald is the only actor in history to win a Tony in all four performance categories (lead, featured, male, female). When she plays this "greatest mother role in musical theatre," the significance speaks for itself.

<!-- fact: McDonald is the only actor to win Tonys in all four performance categories -->

Her "Rose's Turn"—that song hailed as one of the greatest solos in musical theatre—she sings with both explosive power and fragility, perfectly capturing Mama Rose's total collapse at the moment her dream shatters.

<!-- fact: 'Rose's Turn' is Mama Rose's famous Act II solo -->

## Sondheim's Early Genius

Gypsy's lyrics are by Stephen Sondheim, its music by Jule Styne.

<!-- fact: Gypsy's lyrics are by Sondheim, music by Styne -->

Though Sondheim's greatest works came later, Gypsy already shows his astonishing command of words and drama. The blind optimism of "Everything's Coming Up Roses" and the total despair of "Rose's Turn" are two faces of the same coin.

<!-- fact: 'Everything's Coming Up Roses' is the Act I finale song -->

## Why Listen

The 2024 revival, starring Audra McDonald and an all-star cast, earned high critical acclaim.

<!-- fact: The 2024 revival starring Audra McDonald earned high critical acclaim -->

This soundtrack is a precious record of McDonald's take on this legendary role. Whether you're a Sondheim fan, a musical theatre fan, or a listener who wants to see the power of "six Tonys," this is an unmissable album.
