## Before It All Began

October 9, 1986. Her Majesty's Theatre, London.

<!-- fact: The Phantom of the Opera premiered at Her Majesty's Theatre, London, on October 9, 1986 -->

The chandelier had not yet risen. No one knew this show would become one of the longest-running musicals in history; no one knew that the cast recording made months later would make the whole world remember that half-mask before ever entering a theatre.

<!-- fact: The original cast recording was released by Polydor in February 1987; the Abbey Road remaster (Decca 543 928-2) followed in 2001 and is today's streaming version -->

## Crawford's Phantom

Michael Crawford's Phantom is unlike any Phantom since.

<!-- fact: Crawford (b. 1942) was the original London Phantom and won the 1988 Tony Award for Best Actor on Broadway -->

His voice holds a fragility—"The Music of the Night" sounds like a plea rather than a command. This is not a monster luring a girl; it's a man rejected by the world, believing for the first time that someone might stay.

<!-- fiction: literary description of the interpretation -->

Fifteen years later, on the 25th Anniversary stage at the Royal Albert Hall, he stood smiling without singing, while a new generation of Phantoms took up his song.

<!-- fact: At the 2011 Royal Albert Hall 25th Anniversary finale, Crawford appeared but did not sing -->

We've written about that night elsewhere. This recording—is where it all began.

<!-- opinion: positioning of the two recordings -->

## Brightman's Christine

Sarah Brightman's high note in "Think of Me" was a miracle of 1986.

<!-- fact: Brightman was the original Christine in both London and Broadway -->

She was Webber's wife then, his personally chosen Christine. Critics called it nepotism; but four decades later, when people speak of this role, hers is still the first name that comes to mind.

<!-- fact: Brightman and Webber were married 1984–1990; Christine was written for her voice -->

She later left the theatre for a wider world—but every time she returns to sing opera selections, there's still that girl from the dressing room in her voice.

<!-- fiction: sentimental reading of her career -->

## Three Phantoms in Taiwan

The production itself has stepped onto Taiwanese soil three times: 2006 at the National Theater, 2020 at Taipei Arena, and the 2026 island-wide tour.

<!-- fact: The Phantom production visited Taiwan in 2006 (National Theater), 2020 (Taipei Arena), and the 2026 tour -->

And Brightman herself had just finished concerts at Taipei Music Center in July 2025. The original cast didn't come along—but their voices arrived long ago, through this album.

<!-- fact: Sarah Brightman performed 'A Starlight Symphony' at Taipei Music Center July 19–20, 2025 -->
