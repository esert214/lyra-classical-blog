## Winter Candlelight in the Deep Fjords

When Lise Davidsen—hailed as a once-in-a-century dramatic soprano—sings carols from her native Norway, she delivers not the gaudy cheer of shopping malls, but the profound hush of an ancient wooden chapel nestled beneath Nordic snowdrifts.

The reigning heroine of the Metropolitan Opera and Bayreuth tucks away the earth-shattering Wagnerian armor of Brünnhilde and Sieglinde. Instead, she wraps us in vocal warmth as comforting as hot cocoa by an open hearth.

<!-- fact: Released in November 2023 by Decca Classics, recorded with the Norwegian Radio Orchestra in Oslo -->

## Sacred Canvas of Pure White Snow

In *Stille Nacht* and Adolphe Adam's *O Holy Night*, Davidsen’s dynamic mastery is awe-inspiring. Her floating pianissimos hover like frost crystals in freezing air before bursting into a glorious, radiant sunrise that fills the sonic landscape without a hint of strain.

Equally unforgettable is the traditional Scandinavian hymn *Mitt hjerte alltid vanker* ("My Heart Always Wanders to the Manger in Bethlehem"), tinged with evocative Nordic melancholy accompanied by gentle woodwinds and harp.

<!-- fact: Davidsen burst onto the global scene in 2015 winning first prize at Plácido Domingo's Operalia, hailed as the true heir to Kirsten Flagstad -->

## A Fireplace for Weary Souls

Light a single candle on a cold winter evening and let Davidsen’s luminous voice fill the room. This album is spiritual balm—washing away year-end anxieties and placing back in your palms the primal stillness of peace on earth.
