## A Gem of Czech Chamber Music

When we speak of Czech music, we think of Dvořák and Smetana. But 20th-century Czech music holds a gem often overlooked—Martinů's string quartets.

<!-- opinion: commentary on Martinů's place in Czech music -->

Martinů wrote seven string quartets in his life, the best gateway into his chamber world.

<!-- fact: Martinů wrote seven string quartets -->

This album by the Pavel Haas Quartet, containing Nos. 2, 3, 5, and 7, is hailed as "the strongest contemporary recording of this repertoire."

## The Pavel Haas Quartet

The Pavel Haas Quartet, named after Czech composer Pavel Haas, was founded in 2002 and has won multiple Gramophone magazine awards.

<!-- fact: The Pavel Haas Quartet is named after Pavel Haas, founded in 2002, multiple Gramophone award winners -->

They have an innate understanding of Czech music—that complex emotion blending nostalgia, passion, and modernism, which flows naturally in their playing.

## Martinů's Musical Language

Martinů's string quartets span different phases of his creative life—from early folk style to late modernism.

<!-- fact: Martinů's string quartets span different phases of his career -->

No. 2 is full of Czech folk vitality; No. 5 carries the melancholy and complexity of his exile period. The four works together read like a map of 20th-century Czech music.
